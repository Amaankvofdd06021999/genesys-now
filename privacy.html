01

Introduction

Genesys Now ("we," "our," or "us") is a digital systems agency committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at genesysnow.com or engage with our services.

By accessing or using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our site.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as certain information collected automatically when you visit our website.

Information you provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company name submitted through our contact form
  • Project details and service interests you describe in consultation requests
  • Communications you send us via email or other channels

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your consultation requests and inquiries
  • To communicate project scope, proposals, and updates
  • To provide and improve our services
  • To send relevant business communications you have requested
  • To analyze website performance and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

04

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand how visitors interact with the site — data is aggregated and anonymized

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain site functionality.

05

Third-Party Services

Our website integrates a limited set of third-party services to deliver a functional and performant experience:

  • Custom Fonts: The MartianBThai typeface is self-hosted on our servers. No third-party font provider is used and no data is shared.
  • GSAP (GreenSock): Animation library loaded from Cloudflare CDN. No personal data is collected.
  • Lenis: Smooth scroll library loaded via CDN. No personal data is collected.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

06

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS transmission for all data in transit
  • Access controls limiting who can view submitted contact information
  • Regular review of data handling practices

While we take data security seriously, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months unless an ongoing client relationship is established
  • Client project data is retained for the duration of the engagement plus 5 years for legal and tax purposes
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us
08

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
